\section{Training Behavior}

The trainer records loss and accuracy after every epoch, selects the lowest validation-loss checkpoint, restores it, and evaluates the test set once. Linear stopped after epoch 30 and restored epoch 20; MLP stopped after epoch 41 and restored epoch 31; CNN stopped after epoch 65 and restored epoch 55; GRU stopped after epoch 63 and restored epoch 53.

Validation accuracy is often higher than training accuracy. This is expected because training accuracy is measured while random augmentation and dropout are active, whereas validation uses clean inputs and evaluation mode. The curves do not show a sustained validation-loss explosion; instead, early stopping prevents later fluctuations from replacing the best checkpoint. CNN converges to the strongest validation result, while Linear reaches a clear representational plateau.
