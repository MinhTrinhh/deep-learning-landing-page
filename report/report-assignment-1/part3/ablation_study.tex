\section{Ablation Study}

The handbook recommends, but does not require, an ablation for Assignment 1. No matched-pair ablation was recorded in the available outputs, so no ablation result is claimed. The four-model comparison is a controlled architecture study rather than a strict ablation because representation, parameter count, and computation change together.

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \caption{Ablation status and reproducible follow-up experiments.}
    \label{tab:ablation}
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lXX@{}}
        \toprule
        Factor & Current evidence & Required controlled follow-up \\
        \midrule
        Augmentation & All reported models use the augmented pipeline. & Retrain each model with \texttt{--no-aug} under the same seed and compare accuracy/F1. \\
        Dropout & MLP, CNN, and GRU use $p=0.2$. & Retrain with dropout set to zero while fixing every other setting. \\
        GRU row order & Only top-to-bottom rows are evaluated. & Compare rows, columns, and reversed row sequences with hidden size fixed. \\
        Parameter budget & Architectures have different sizes. & Match parameter counts to separate inductive bias from capacity. \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabularx}
\end{table}

These experiments are future work and must not be interpreted as completed results.
