\section{Quantitative Results}

All reported values in Tables~\ref{tab:predictive-results} and~\ref{tab:resource-results} come from the single augmented runs recorded in \path{Assignment1/outputs/log.txt} with seed 42. Metrics are computed on the official 10,000-image test set after restoring the minimum-validation-loss checkpoint. The log includes complete predictive and resource results for all five architectures.

CNN is the most accurate and has the best macro-F1, while Linear is smallest and fastest. GRU is the most parameter-efficient nonlinear model: it exceeds MLP by 2.56 accuracy points with 89.1\% fewer parameters. ViT reaches 87.93\% accuracy and 0.8771 macro-F1, slightly below GRU, while using about 13 times as many parameters and taking roughly 5.9 times as long to train. ViT inference is about 6.9 times slower than GRU (0.264764 ms/sample) and has the highest FLOPs (0.053043 billion/sample). The strong accuracy but high compute cost illustrates that global attention does not automatically improve this small-image task. Values are single-run measurements rather than mean $\pm$ standard deviation, and timing comparisons are subject to the hardware-recording limitation described in Table~\ref{tab:training-setup}.
