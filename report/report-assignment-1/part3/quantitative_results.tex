\section{Quantitative Results}

All values in Tables~\ref{tab:predictive-results} and~\ref{tab:resource-results} come from the single augmented run recorded in \texttt{Assignment1/outputs/log.txt} with seed 42. Metrics are computed on the official 10,000-image test set after restoring the minimum-validation-loss checkpoint.

    \caption{Predictive performance on the Fashion-MNIST test set.}

    \begin{tabular}{@{}lrrrrr@{}}
        \toprule
        Method & Test loss & Accuracy & Macro precision & Macro recall & Macro-F1 \\
        \midrule
        Linear & 0.7660 & 0.7378 & 0.7305 & 0.7378 & 0.7286 \\
        MLP & 0.3756 & 0.8580 & 0.8564 & 0.8580 & 0.8560 \\
        CNN & \textbf{0.2069} & \textbf{0.9255} & \textbf{0.9272} & \textbf{0.9255} & \textbf{0.9259} \\
        GRU & 0.3130 & 0.8836 & 0.8829 & 0.8836 & 0.8821 \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}

CNN is the most accurate and has the best macro-F1, while Linear is smallest and fastest. GRU is the most parameter-efficient nonlinear model: it exceeds MLP by 2.56 accuracy points with 89.1\% fewer parameters. Its sequential execution nevertheless makes inference slower than MLP. Values are single-run measurements rather than mean $\pm$ standard deviation, and timing comparisons are subject to the hardware-recording limitation described in Table~\ref{tab:training-setup}.
