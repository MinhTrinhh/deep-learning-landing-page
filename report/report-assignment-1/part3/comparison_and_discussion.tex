\section{Comparison and Discussion}

\textbf{Linear / softmax.} The baseline reaches 73.78\% accuracy and 0.7286 macro-F1 with only 7,850 parameters. Its additive pixel scoring is computationally efficient but cannot express nonlinear interactions or reusable local patterns. The very low Shirt F1 (0.3040) is direct evidence of under-capacity for overlapping garment categories.

\textbf{MLP.} Adding nonlinear dense layers raises accuracy by 12.02 percentage points and macro-F1 by 0.1274 over Linear. This supports the hypothesis that nonlinear combinations of pixels are necessary. The cost is 567,434 parameters and slower inference, while the flattened representation still has no explicit locality or translation prior.

\textbf{GRU.} Row-sequence processing reaches 88.36\% accuracy and 0.8821 macro-F1, improving on MLP by 2.56 accuracy points and 0.0261 macro-F1 while using about one-ninth as many parameters. Shared recurrent weights encode dependencies between image rows efficiently. However, sequential computation makes GRU training 1.16 times and inference 2.12 times as slow as MLP in the recorded run. Its top-to-bottom ordering also treats horizontal and vertical relationships asymmetrically.

\textbf{CNN.} CNN ranks first at 92.55\% accuracy and 0.9259 macro-F1. Local $3\times3$ filters, shared weights, and pooling match the two-dimensional image structure and improve Shirt F1 to 0.7762. Relative to GRU, CNN gains 4.19 accuracy points and 0.0438 macro-F1, but uses 27.3 times as many parameters and takes 1.54 times as long to train.

Overall, all four experimental hypotheses are supported: nonlinear dense features improve on a linear boundary; a compact row-sequence model improves further; and the image-specific spatial bias of CNN gives the best predictive result. The preferred model nevertheless depends on the objective. CNN is best for test quality, Linear for minimum latency and size, and GRU offers the strongest accuracy--parameter compromise. Timing evidence is weaker than predictive evidence because the precise CPU environment was not fully recorded. No conclusion can yet be drawn about Transformer performance.
