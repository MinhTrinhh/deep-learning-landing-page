\section{Preprocessing}

Images are converted to tensors and normalized using statistics calculated from the 54,000-image training split. Training images additionally receive random horizontal flips and small affine transformations; validation and test images use deterministic preprocessing only.

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \caption{Preprocessing pipeline.}
    \label{tab:preprocessing}
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lXX@{}}
        \toprule
        Stage & Operation & Configuration \\
        \midrule
        Cleaning & No additional cleaning & Images and labels are read through torchvision; no missing or invalid records were detected. \\
        Tensor conversion & \texttt{ToTensor()} & Converts \texttt{uint8} pixels from $[0,255]$ to floating point in $[0,1]$ with shape $[B,1,28,28]$. \\
        Normalization & $(x-\mu_{\mathrm{train}})/\sigma_{\mathrm{train}}$ & Training-split statistics are reused unchanged for validation and test data. \\
        Augmentation & Horizontal flip and affine transform & Training only: flip probability 0.5; rotation $[-10,10]^{\circ}$; translation 0.1; scale $[0.9,1.1]$. \\
        Linear / MLP input & Flatten internally & $[B,1,28,28]\rightarrow[B,784]$. \\
        CNN input & Preserve spatial grid & $[B,1,28,28]$. \\
        GRU input & Remove channel axis & $[B,1,28,28]\rightarrow[B,28,28]$: 28 row timesteps with 28 features each. \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabularx}
\end{table}

Augmentation is intentionally excluded from validation and test data. Consequently, validation accuracy may exceed training accuracy because validation uses clean inputs and disables dropout, while training metrics are measured on augmented inputs with dropout active.
