\section{Preprocessing}

Images are converted to tensors and normalized using the training-split statistics. Training images additionally receive random horizontal flips and small affine transformations; validation and test images use deterministic preprocessing.

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \caption{Preprocessing pipeline.}
    \label{tab:preprocessing}
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lXX@{}}
        \toprule
        Stage & Operation & Configuration \\
        \midrule
        Cleaning & No additional cleaning & Fashion-MNIST images and labels are consumed through torchvision. \\
        Transformation & `ToTensor` & Converts images to floating-point tensors with shape $1\times28\times28$. \\
        Normalization & Train-split mean and standard deviation & Statistics are computed on training images only, then applied to train, validation, and test inputs. \\
        Augmentation & Random flip and affine transform & Training only: horizontal flip with $p=0.5$; rotation $[-10,10]^{\circ}$, translation 0.1, scale $[0.9,1.1]$. \\
        Tensor shape & $1\times28\times28$ (then 784 features) & The linear and MLP models flatten the image inside `forward`. \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabularx}
\end{table}
